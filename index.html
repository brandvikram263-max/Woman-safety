🛡️ Women Safety

Stay safe • Stay aware • Stay connected

🚨 Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger, contact a trusted person or local emergency services.

🚨 CALL EMERGENCY — 112

📞 Important Contacts

🚨 Emergency: 112 ☎️ Women Helpline: 181 ☎️ Women Police Helpline: 1091

🛡️ Safety Tips

  • Keep your phone charged when travelling.
  • Tell a trusted person about your travel plans.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Avoid isolated places when possible.
  • Save important emergency contacts.
  • If something feels unsafe, move toward a safe public place and seek help.

🤝 Get Help

In an emergency, try to reach a trusted adult, family member, teacher, security staff, police, or another responsible person nearby.
